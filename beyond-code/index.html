01 / Trekking

Happiest somewhere above the tree line

Growing up in Nepal, the Himalaya were always on the horizon. Trekking is how I reset — long days on foot, simple food, cold mornings and views that make every climb worth it. Some trails are famous; others, like Badimalika in the far west, see very few trekkers.
